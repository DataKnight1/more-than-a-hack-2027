## A. Ball touches and possession changes

Turnovers (team→team) with the ball seen ±0.5 s: 4,339. Detected touch candidates: 44.6 per minute of seen live ball; 60% of turnovers have a touch candidate within ±0.24 s.

Rule *'possession changes when the nearest player (< 3 m) at a touch belongs to the other team'*, ±0.5 s tolerance: recall 0.43, precision 0.27 (6,990 predicted changes).

## E. Carries / dribbles (same player ≤ 1.5 m from the ball for ≥ 1 s at > 3 m/s)

n 3,288; median duration 1.9 s, carrier speed 4.1 m/s, forward speed 2.4 m/s; within 1 s after the carry ends the opponent has the ball in 14% and the ball is dead in 5% of cases.


## Findings (written 2026-11-07 before any feature-v3 model was trained)

1. **Touches mark possession changes.** Ball acceleration peaks at the change (≈ 32–37 m/s² vs
   ≈ 10 in open play); 60 % of turnovers have a detected touch within ±0.24 s. A pure rule —
   *possession changes when the nearest player at a touch is from the other team* — already finds
   43 % of turnovers (precision 0.27): a useful, frame-accurate cue for the network, not a model.
2. **Short (< 2 s) possessions are contested balls.** The losing team stays close (2.5 m vs 4.0 m,
   AUC 0.33), more players are within 3 m, the ball keeps moving fast after the change (7.2 vs
   5.0 m/s): deflections, touch-and-lose, loose balls. The new team's player is at the ball at
   t = 0 but 4.2 m away 1 s later (vs 2.4 m when the possession lasts).
3. **Pressure on the carrier predicts turnovers monotonically.** P(opponent has the ball within 2 s)
   rises from 1.4 % (nearest opponent > 10 m) to 26 % (< 1 m); from 3.9 % (no opponent within
   5 m) to 26 % (3+).
4. **Marking and roles.** In possession the striker is marked tightly (3.3–3.7 m vs 5.0–5.4 m,
   AUC 0.34–0.36 on all three providers), the team is further from its markers (AUC 0.63–0.69),
   full-backs are wider (AUC 0.60–0.66) and higher (Metrica/SkillCorner), the last defender is
   higher (SkillCorner). SkillCorner role values are inflated by the broadcast view.
5. **No lead-lag at 0.4 s.** Whether the nearest opponent copies a player's movement 0.4 s later
   does not separate attackers from defenders (AUC 0.48–0.54): players and markers move together
   (cosine ≈ 0.8) with no measurable lag at this horizon. Not used as a feature.
6. **Counter-pressing shortens the next possession.** With 0 losing-team players within 10 m of
   the ball 1 s after the loss, the new possession lasts a median 11.2 s; with 3+, 3.2 s (34 %
   under 2 s). Recoveries in the attacking third are short (median 2.7 s, 40 % < 2 s); in the
   defensive and middle thirds 4.5–4.6 s.
7. **After a recovery the recovering team expands** (+0.47 m lateral spread in 3 s) and the
   losing team compacts (−0.59 m).
8. **Carries.** Dribbles/carries (≥ 1 s, > 3 m/s) last a median 1.9 s at 4.1 m/s; 14 % end with
   the opponent on the ball within 1 s.

Findings 1–4 and 6 are encoded as feature version 3 (`src/possession_lab/neural/individual_features.py`).
