## Findings (interpretation, 2026-11-06)

1. **Team width is the strongest ball-independent possession signal.** The team in possession
   is wider (median 13.6 vs 11.3 m on IDSSE) — AUC 0.84–0.88 on all three providers and still
   0.83 on ball-unseen frames (degraded IDSSE). Length and forward position help less (0.54–0.69).
2. **Ball proximity is the sharpest signal when the ball is seen:** nearest player of the team in
   possession 0.5–2 m vs ~5 m (AUC 0.16–0.18, i.e. 0.82–0.84 inverted); players within 3 m 0.76–0.79.
3. **Direction of movement:** the possessing team's centroid moves forward (+0.5 m/s) while the
   defenders drop (−0.5 m/s) — AUC 0.59–0.72; share of players running forward 0.58–0.69.
4. **Defenders press:** the three nearest defenders close on the ball faster (0.7–1.6 m/s vs
   ~0–1 m/s; AUC 0.19–0.37 inverted) and the defending team's mean speed is higher (AUC 0.27–0.37).
   Sprint share alone barely separates possession (≈0.5).
5. **Possession changes:** the new possessor's nearest player arrives at the ball exactly at the
   change (distance −3 m, players within 5 m +0.5) — the precise *timing* signal; team shape
   (width, forward velocity, forward running) flips 1–2 s *after* the change — a slower
   confirmation signal usable by a bidirectional model.
6. **Dead ball:** ball speed → 0 (AUC 0.91–0.995), collective slow-down from ~1 s before (all-player
   mean speed AUC 0.85–0.93), ball near a line; a ball-speed spike precedes the ball going out.
7. **Provider artefact (data-cleaning action):** SkillCorner dead-ball frames often have *no
   detected players* (broadcast replays/cut-aways), so all team features read 0 and "ball unseen
   for ~7 s" marks dead ball. A model could learn "nothing visible ⇒ dead", which does not hold for
   a fixed tactical camera. Frames with too few detected players should be excluded from the loss.
8. Ball-unseen AUCs collapse to ~0.5 on SkillCorner/Metrica natural missingness (the ball is mostly
   missing during dead ball / replays there), so their "unseen" columns describe dead-ball
   artefacts more than occlusion; the degraded-IDSSE column is the cleaner occlusion test.

**Design consequences:** add explicit, direction-relative team-context features (width, length,
centroid forward velocity, forward-running share, pressure counts, closing speeds, collective
speed, ball speed / near-line) as an extra per-frame token; add per-player direction-relative
features (forward velocity, closing speed towards the ball); and mask low-detection frames from
training.
